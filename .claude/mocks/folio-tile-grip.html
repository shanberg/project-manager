The tile grip as the tab's controls

A space with its sidebar folded, two tiles. Rest the pointer on a grip at the top centre of a tile (or click it, depending on the setting) and the tab's controls unfold from the middle, under the grip. The grip stays where it is: it still drags the tile, and clicking it again closes them. Move away and they close. Click the address to edit it. All pages here are pictures.
