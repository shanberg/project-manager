Spaces browser motion

A space window, as built. Each interaction can be set to Now (what the app does) or Proposed. Click rows, close them with ×, fold the Trip folder, hover the sidebar for Clear, ⇧-click the page's link. Slow motion runs everything at 4× length.

Rest on a tile's grip (the bar at its top centre) or click it for the capsule. With the sidebar folded, move to the window's left edge. With the window focused: S folds the sidebar, 1–9 as ⌘1–⌘9, N next card, T new tab, Esc closes the preview, Return opens it as a card.
