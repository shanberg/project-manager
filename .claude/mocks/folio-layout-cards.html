A card in a layout, and in the sidebar

Rule: an unpinned card lives in one place, Today or one layout. Using a card in a second layout pins it, and each layout lists it as a reference. Layouts are always pinned. Try a row's ··· menu (or right-click): Add to Layout, Close, Move to Today, Unpin. Click a layout to open it; click a card under it to bring that tile forward.

Decided

  • Unpinned cards: one home, Today or one layout.
  • A second layout pins the card.
  • Layouts sit in Pinned. Notes behaves as a pinned card.
  • Close: unpinned off the board (toast); pinned only leaves that layout.
